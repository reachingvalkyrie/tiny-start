      Help

      How it works

      1. Pick who’s studying and type a topic.
      2. Follow the small steps. Tick each one off.
      3. Run the timer, then get an explanation and practice questions.
      For parents

      Choose “I’m helping my child.” Each step then shows a tip on how to guide them. Sit beside them, let them teach it back to you, and praise effort over right answers.

      No quiet place?

      Try headphones with soft background sound, a library or school room, or a corner with your back to the room. Even 5 minutes with your phone out of reach counts.

      No device, notes, or books?

      Study from memory: say aloud what you remember, write it on any paper, then check it against class notes later. Ask a classmate to share photos of notes.

      Feeling overwhelmed?

      Pick the smallest step and ignore the rest. Finishing one step is a win. If this feels bigger than stress, talk to a teacher, counselor, or someone you trust.

      Reading or focus is hard?

      Turn on the easy-read font and bigger text in Settings. Break text into short chunks, use a ruler under the line, and ask your school about learning support.
